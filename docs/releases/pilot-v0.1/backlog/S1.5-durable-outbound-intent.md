# S1.5 — Durable Outbound Telegram Intent


## Goal

Persist outbound Telegram intent and delivery attempts so retries do not repeat internal business mutations and external ambiguity is explicit.

## Required behavior

- Durable outbound intent is committed with required state/messages/events before send.
- Delivery worker sends from durable intent.
- Attempts, known result, provider identifiers, failure, and ambiguity are persisted.
- Retry does not repeat the originating internal mutation.
- External Telegram exactly-once is not claimed.
- Network-ambiguous send enters explicit ambiguity policy.
- Stale lease/CAS owner cannot create/send a final intent.

## In scope

Outbound intent schema/repository, delivery worker or accepted equivalent, adapter integration, retry/ambiguity policy, idempotency linkage, tests and observability.

## Out of scope

Provider-supported exactly-once guarantee, S2 tenant fix, S3 close idempotency, general notification platform.

## Task acceptance criteria

- S1.5-AC-01: internal commit exists before outbound attempt.
- S1.5-AC-02: send failure retries from same intent without repeating internal mutation.
- S1.5-AC-03: success records delivery result and does not resend normally.
- S1.5-AC-04: timeout after possible provider success records ambiguity and follows documented policy.
- S1.5-AC-05: duplicate inbox processing cannot create two active intents for one logical effect.
- S1.5-AC-06: stale/conflicted processor creates no final intent.

## Required QA design

Cover failure before send, during send, after provider success/before DB result, restart, duplicate worker claim, provider 4xx/5xx/rate limit, retry exhaustion, and operator reconciliation.

## Expected test levels

Repository integration, delivery-worker integration with fake Telegram adapter, ambiguous network simulation, end-to-end internal mutation assertion.

## Rollback/degraded behavior

Pause outbound delivery while preserving accepted work and intents. Operator/manager reconciliation is allowed. Never delete ambiguous intents or repeat business mutations to force delivery.

## Documentation updates

R25 remains residual and bounded, not closed. Update actual outbound flow, evidence, traceability, and operator policy.
